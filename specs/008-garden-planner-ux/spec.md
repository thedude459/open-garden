# Feature Specification: Garden Planner UX

**Feature Branch**: `008-garden-planner-ux`

**Created**: 2026-08-18

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "The garden planning functionality is not very good. It does not show the beds currently in the garden, there is not way to drag and drop plantings. The functionality is not user friendly and the performance is not very good currently. The UI should be more like this but not as cartoony - https://planter.garden/app/gardens"

Addendum (2026-08-21): Add a Garden Visualization with two views: a whole-garden overview and a per-bed planting view. Garden Overview shows placed beds and non-planting areas as rectangles and allows add/move/resize/remove of those shapes; it shows plantings only at a glance and MUST NOT add or edit plantings. Selecting a bed opens Bed View, where plantings are added, edited, and removed. Bed View MUST NOT create, resize, move, or delete the bed or add non-planting areas. Bed geometry is owned only by Overview; plantings are owned only by Bed View. Non-planting areas exist only on Overview and never contain plantings.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-08-18

- Q: Unsized beds on the plan — where do named beds without length and width appear? → A: When creating a bed, the user must specify the size of the bed (no unsized beds on the plan)
- Q: When a drag is kept — when does a drop become the saved garden plan other members see? → A: Explicit Save: drags update a local draft; Save commits when there are no spacing or fit flags; other members see the last successful save
- Q: How to unplace a planting — empty plan space does not unplace; what does? → A: **Superseded 2026-08-21.** There is no Unplace action. Remove **transplant** from the bed (explicit Remove from bed, or drop onto the planting tray) restores it to the tray with prior information. Remove **direct seed** from the bed (confirm) deletes that planting. Dropping a direct seed on the tray MUST NOT silently delete it.
- Q: Pan the plan vs move a bed — how do those gestures stay distinct? → A: Empty space pans; dragging a bed or planting moves that object (pinch or zoom controls still change scale)
- Q: When spacing flags update — live during drag, after drop, or block the drop? → A: Flags when the gesture ends: drop or resize completes, then flags refresh on the draft before Save
- Q: Is confirmed bed delete draft-until-Save? → A: No. Confirmed bed delete stays immediate (same as Garden Layout Designer). Create/move/place stay draft until Save.
- Q: Do offline edits apply to the draft? → A: No. Create, drag, move, resize, rotate, remove-from-bed, delete, and Save show online-required and MUST NOT change the draft or stored plan.
- Q: If Save POSTs new beds then PUT fails? → A: Remove those POSTed beds so the Save does not leave unsized name-only rows. The in-memory draft may keep the sized beds for a later Save.
- Q: Unknown-spacing planting mark size? → A: Visual radius 6 inches; MUST NOT be treated as catalog spacing for flags or Save.
- Q: How is an unsaved draft discarded? → A: Leaving the planner page or reloading discards the in-memory draft. The next open shows the last successful save. No separate Discard control.

### Session 2026-08-21

- Q: One planner canvas vs two views — where are beds arranged vs where are plantings placed? → A: Two views. **Garden Overview** owns bed identity and geometry (rectangular position and dimensions) and non-planting areas. **Bed View** owns that bed’s plantings. Overview MUST NOT add or edit plantings. Bed View MUST NOT create, resize, move, or delete the bed or add non-planting areas.
- Q: What does Overview show for plantings? → A: Name (and count) labels on or next to each occupied bed — enough to see what is in the garden, not in-bed positions. Selecting a bed navigates to its Bed View.
- Q: What are non-planting areas? → A: Simple rectangles (width, height, x/y) on Garden Overview only — walkways, paths, structures, and similar. They are visually distinct from beds and never contain plantings.
- Q: Does navigating Overview ↔ Bed View discard the draft? → A: No. Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View share one working **layout** draft. Only leaving the garden planner entirely or reloading discards the in-memory layout draft. Transplant create/full-delete is not that draft.
- Q: How does a planting move from one bed to another? → A: Overview MUST NOT reassign plantings by dragging them between beds. A transplant can be removed from bed A (it returns to the tray with its information) and then placed from the tray into bed B. A direct-seed planting cannot move via the tray: removing it from a bed deletes it; planting in another bed is a new direct seed.
- Q: Select a bed on Overview — open Bed View, or type length/width/origin on the map? → A: Length and width are entered on a **Create new bed** action (required at create). After create, the bed appears on Overview and can be moved, resized, and rotated there; resize may still change length/width. Selecting a bed opens Bed View to add plantings and does not open a size inspector.
- Q: What does the Overview planting summary look like? → A: Each occupied bed shows planting **names** (and a **count** when the same name appears more than once) on or next to the bed. Overview MUST NOT show position-accurate plant marks.
- Q: Which plantings appear in the Bed View planting tray, and how is it filled? → A: The tray shows only indoor-started **transplant** (future) plantings that have no bed placement. **Transplant View** is a separate view for those plantings, including water and fertilizer tasks (direct-seed plantings do not get those indoor-start tasks). **New** tray records are created and fully deleted only in Transplant View. Bed View may drag a tray item onto the open bed to place it (it then has a placement and leaves the tray). Direct seed in Bed View picks any catalog plant and places it in this bed; it never enters the tray. Removing a **transplant** from a bed returns it to the tray and keeps its prior information. Removing a **direct seed** from a bed deletes that planting.
- Q: If a planting is already in a bed, how is it removed? → A: Remove **direct seed** from the bed and the planting is gone (deleted). Remove **transplant** from the bed and it returns to the planting tray and keeps all the information it had previously (including indoor-start water and fertilizer tasks). This restore is not creating a new tray record.
- Q: Where does a newly created bed or non-planting area appear on Overview? → A: After **Create new bed** (or creating a non-planting area) with name, length, and width, the rectangle **appears in the currently visible Overview area** (for example the center of the current view). The gardener then moves, resizes, or rotates it. A second click-to-place step is not required.
- Q: Is deleting a non-planting area draft-until-Save or immediate? → A: **Confirmed delete is immediate** (same pattern as beds). Create, move, and resize of non-planting areas stay draft until Save. Delete does not affect beds or plantings.

### Session 2026-08-21 (analysis)

- Q: What happens to leftover name-only beds (`geometry` null) from before Create-requires-size? → A: They MUST NOT be sized in this feature. Migration `0008` **deletes** every unsized bed. Direct-seed plantings on those beds are deleted; transplant plantings are unassigned and unplaced (return to the tray, keep indoor info). After migrate, the API MUST refuse any name-only bed create. At migrate time existing plantings default to `direct_seed`, so leftover-bed plantings are deleted.
- Q: How are indoor water/fertilizer tasks created? → A: **Derived** from catalog water/fertilizer intervals (`deriveIndoorReminders`) for unplaced transplants only. Omit a kind when that interval is null. Complete/dismiss like Care Reminders (006). No custom indoor task editor and no opt-out beyond complete/dismiss.
- Q: How does the gardener tell a Bed View drop missed the bed? → A: Visible status / `aria-live` message (e.g. **Drop missed a bed**). The planting stays in the tray or its previous position.
- Q: Zoom on Overview and Bed View? → A: **Zoom in** / **Zoom out** controls and pinch on Overview; Bed View MAY share the same canvas zoom. Viewers can pan and zoom; they MUST NOT move beds, areas, or plantings.
- Q: How is the 3-second Overview ready target verified? → A: **Manual** quickstart gate on the required 10×50 fixture. Not a Playwright timing assertion.
- Q: Duplicate non-planting-area names? → A: Unique among **areas** in that garden (beds MAY reuse an area’s name). Empty name → 400; duplicate area name → 409.
- Q: Who owns the offline layout cache? → A: `garden-layout-cache.service.ts` is the only IndexedDB writer (last successful GET). The planner draft is memory-only and hydrates from that cache or a fresh GET.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - See the Whole Garden on Overview (Priority: P1)

A garden owner, collaborator, or viewer opens the Garden Overview and immediately sees a visual map of the garden: every sized named bed that has been placed, every non-planting area, and a name (and count) summary of current plantings in each occupied bed. Beds and non-planting areas are simple rectangles defined by width and height and placed at an x/y position so relative size and position match the real garden well enough to use as a map. Empty beds still appear. Nothing that exists as a sized placed bed is missing. Plantings on Overview are labels only — the gardener cannot add, move, or remove plantings from this view, and in-bed positions are not drawn here.

**Why this priority**: Gardeners cannot plan if they cannot see the garden they already have. Missing beds was the first failure of the current planner. The overview is the map; planting detail belongs one level down.

**Independent Test**: With a garden that has several sized placed beds (including one empty bed), some plantings in beds, and at least one non-planting area, open Overview and confirm every sized placed bed and every non-planting area is visible at relative size and position, occupied beds show planting name (and count) labels, empty beds still show with no planting names, a viewer sees the same map and cannot edit it, and a non-member cannot open it (same not-found outcome as a missing garden).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a garden with several named beds that have length, width, and a position, **When** a member opens Garden Overview, **Then** every one of those beds is visible as a rectangle on the visual map (not only in a separate list).
2. **Given** a garden with at least one non-planting area (for example a path or structure), **When** a member opens Garden Overview, **Then** that area is visible as a rectangle distinct from beds, at its stored size and position.
3. **Given** beds with current plantings, **When** a member looks at Overview, **Then** each occupied bed shows the planting names (and a count when the same name appears more than once) on or next to the bed, and Overview does not draw those plantings at in-bed positions.
4. **Given** an empty named bed (no plantings) that has a size and position, **When** a member opens Overview, **Then** the empty bed is still shown on the map.
5. **Given** Garden Overview, **When** an owner or collaborator tries to add, move, or remove a planting on that view, **Then** they cannot — placing seeds or plants into a bed is not available here.
6. **Given** a viewer, **When** they open Overview, **Then** they see the same beds, areas, and planting name (and count) labels as other members and cannot add, hide, resize, or delete beds or non-planting areas.
7. **Given** User B is not a member, **When** they are signed in, **Then** they cannot open that garden’s Overview or Bed View (same not-found outcome as a missing garden).

### User Story 2 - Arrange Beds and Non-Planting Areas on Overview (Priority: P1)

An owner or collaborator adds, moves, resizes, rotates, and removes garden beds and non-planting areas on Garden Overview. A **Create new bed** action is how a bed is first sized: the gardener must give a name, length, and width there, then the bed appears on the map. After create, they arrange it on Overview (move, resize, rotate). Selecting a bed opens that bed’s Bed View to add plantings — it is not how length and width are entered. Non-planting areas are also rectangles (width, height, position) and never receive plantings. Dragging on the map is the primary way to arrange the garden after create; a numeric precision path MAY exist for later size or position changes as long as it does not use the same select-to-open gesture.

**Why this priority**: The overview is the only place bed geometry and non-planting areas can be edited. Without this, the two-view split has no layout editor.

**Independent Test**: Use Create new bed with name, length, and width; see it in the visible Overview; move, resize, and rotate it; add a non-planting area in the visible Overview, move and resize it, Save; confirm delete of an area is immediate after confirm; confirm a planting cannot be placed on Overview; select the bed and land in Bed View (not a size form); confirm delete of a bed after confirm; a viewer cannot mutate.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an owner or collaborator, **When** they use **Create new bed** on Overview, **Then** they must provide a name, length, and width on that action; the bed then appears in the currently visible Overview area (for example the center of the current view) on the to-scale map; a create attempt without length or width is refused and no bed is added.
2. **Given** an owner or collaborator and a bed already on Overview, **When** they drag it, **Then** the bed moves and its plantings stay attached (same attach-on-move rule as Garden Layout Designer). **When** they finish resizing it, **Then** length and width are on the draft and fit flags refresh at gesture end; if a planting no longer fits, it stays placed with a fit flag and Save is refused until they fix it in Bed View. **When** they rotate it 90 degrees, **Then** orientation updates on Overview.
3. **Given** an owner or collaborator, **When** they add a non-planting area with a name, length, and width on Overview, **Then** it appears as a rectangle distinct from beds in the currently visible Overview area. **When** they move or resize it, **Then** those changes are on the draft until Save. **When** they delete it and confirm, **Then** it is gone immediately (not draft-until-Save) and beds and plantings are unchanged. **When** they cancel confirm, **Then** the area remains.
4. **Given** Garden Overview, **When** an owner or collaborator selects a bed, **Then** they navigate to that bed’s Bed View to add or manage plantings. Selecting a bed MUST NOT open a length/width inspector instead of Bed View.
5. **Given** an owner or collaborator, **When** they choose to delete a bed from Overview and confirm, **Then** that named bed is gone from the map and the planting list; **direct-seed** plantings that were in that bed are gone; **transplant** plantings that were in that bed return to the tray and keep the information they had. **When** they cancel confirm, **Then** the bed and geometry remain. Confirmed bed delete is immediate (not draft-until-Save).
6. **Given** a member using a keyboard or needing precision after a bed exists, **When** they adjust length, width, or origin as numbers, **Then** they can do so through a precision path that does **not** use select-to-open (Create new bed already collected the first size; select still opens Bed View). Non-planting areas MAY use selection for geometry because they have no Bed View.
7. **Given** a viewer, **When** they open Overview, **Then** they cannot add, move, resize, rotate, or delete beds or non-planting areas. They can still select a bed to open its Bed View as read-only.

### User Story 3 - Manage Indoor Transplants (Priority: P1)

An owner or collaborator opens **Transplant View** to manage indoor-started future plantings separately from beds. They **create new** future plantings and **fully delete** them here. Those new records appear in the Bed View planting tray. A transplant removed from a bed also returns to the tray (restore, not a new record). Indoor water and fertilizer items are **derived** from catalog intervals (complete/dismiss like Care Reminders); a direct-seed planting does not get those indoor-start items. A viewer can read Transplant View but cannot add or fully delete future plantings.

**Why this priority**: The Bed View tray is empty until transplants exist, and indoor starts need care that in-ground direct seed does not.

**Independent Test**: Add a future planting in Transplant View and see it in the Bed View tray with no bed placement; delete it in Transplant View and confirm it is gone from the tray; confirm Bed View cannot create a new tray record or fully delete a transplant record; confirm a transplant removed from a bed returns to the tray with its information; confirm derived water and fertilizer items can be completed/dismissed on Transplant View and are not applied as indoor-start items to a direct-seed planting; a viewer cannot mutate; a non-member cannot open Transplant View (same not-found as a missing garden).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an owner or collaborator of a garden, **When** they open Transplant View, **Then** they see that garden’s future plantings (indoor starts with no bed placement) and can add or delete them.
2. **Given** Transplant View, **When** they add a future planting, **Then** it is not placed in a bed, it appears in the Bed View planting tray, and it can have derived water and fertilizer items (when the catalog has intervals) that they complete/dismiss.
3. **Given** a future planting, **When** they delete it in Transplant View, **Then** it is gone from the tray and is not on a bed. Bed View has no action that **creates** a new tray record or **fully deletes** a transplant record (returning a placed transplant to the tray is restore, not delete).
4. **Given** a direct-seed planting in a bed, **When** a member compares care, **Then** that planting does not receive the indoor-start water and fertilizer tasks that transplants have in Transplant View.
5. **Given** Bed View, **When** an owner or collaborator tries to create a **new** future planting in the tray or fully delete a transplant record, **Then** they cannot — those actions exist only in Transplant View. Returning a placed transplant to the tray is allowed (see Bed View).
6. **Given** a viewer, **When** they open Transplant View, **Then** they can read future plantings and associated water/fertilizer tasks and cannot add or delete them.
7. **Given** User B is not a member, **When** they are signed in, **Then** they cannot open that garden’s Transplant View (same not-found outcome as a missing garden).

### User Story 4 - Manage Plantings in Bed View (Priority: P1)

An owner or collaborator selects a bed on Overview and reaches Bed View for that bed. They see the bed’s rectangular dimensions (width and height) and its internal layout. They add plantings in two ways: **direct seed** — pick any plant from the catalog and place it in this bed (never enters the indoor tray) — or **transplant** — drag a future planting from the planting tray onto the bed. They can reposition plantings in the bed. They can **remove from bed**: a direct-seed planting is then gone; a transplant returns to the tray and keeps the information it had. They cannot create new tray records or fully delete transplant records (Transplant View only). They cannot create, resize, move, or delete the bed itself, and they cannot add non-planting areas. As they drag, the planting follows the pointer. Drops update the shared working draft; an explicit Save commits when there are no spacing or fit flags. Other members still see the last successful save until then. A viewer can see the last saved plantings in the bed but cannot drag them.

**Why this priority**: Placing plants is the reason to open a bed. Direct seed and transplant follow different paths; only transplant uses the tray.

**Independent Test**: With at least one future planting in Transplant View, open Bed View, drag it from the tray onto the bed and Save when valid; direct-seed any catalog plant into the bed; remove the transplant and confirm it is back in the tray with the same information; remove the direct seed and confirm it is gone; confirm Bed View cannot create or fully delete transplant records; confirm Overview cannot place plantings; confirm Bed View has no controls to resize, move, or delete the bed; a viewer cannot drag.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an owner or collaborator on Garden Overview, **When** they select a specific bed, **Then** they reach Bed View for that bed and see its width and height and its internal layout (placed plantings in position).
2. **Given** Bed View and at least one future planting in the tray, **When** they drag that planting from the tray onto the bed and release, **Then** it is placed in that bed at the drop position on the working draft, it no longer appears in the tray (it has a bed placement), and it is visible with its name. Other members still show the last successful save until this editor Saves successfully.
3. **Given** Bed View, **When** they direct-seed by choosing any plant from the catalog and placing it in this bed, **Then** that planting is in this bed on the draft, it is not a future planting, and it never appears in the indoor tray.
4. **Given** a placed planting in this bed, **When** an owner or collaborator drags it to another position in the same bed and releases, **Then** it stays in that bed at the new position on the draft.
5. **Given** a drag in progress in Bed View, **When** the user moves the pointer, **Then** the planting follows the pointer for the whole gesture (it does not stay put until release and then jump).
6. **Given** a drag that would create a spacing or fit problem, **When** the user releases, **Then** the placement is shown at the drop position on the draft and spacing/fit flags refresh at that moment (not continuously during the drag); Save is refused until they fix it; the last successful stored plan is unchanged.
7. **Given** a placed **transplant**, **When** they remove it from the bed (explicit Remove from bed, or drop onto the planting tray), **Then** it returns to the tray on the draft and keeps all the information it had previously (including indoor-start water and fertilizer tasks). It is not deleted.
8. **Given** a placed **direct-seed** planting, **When** they remove it from the bed (confirmed Remove from bed), **Then** that planting is gone (deleted). Dropping a direct-seed planting onto the tray MUST NOT silently delete it; Remove from bed is the delete path and MUST confirm.
9. **Given** Bed View, **When** an owner or collaborator tries to create, resize, move, or delete the bed, or to add a non-planting area, **Then** those actions are not available — they remain Garden Overview responsibilities.
10. **Given** a transplant that should move to a different bed, **When** they remove it from this bed, **Then** it is in the tray with its information kept; **When** they open the destination bed and place it from the tray, **Then** it is in that bed on the draft.
11. **Given** unsaved draft changes in Bed View, **When** the editor returns to Overview or opens Transplant View without leaving the planner, **Then** the shared layout draft is still there. **When** another member opens the planner, **Then** they see the last successful save, not the first editor’s draft.
12. **Given** a valid draft (no spacing or fit flags), **When** the editor Saves from Overview or Bed View, **Then** the stored plan updates and other members see it on next open. **When** they leave the garden planner entirely or reload without a successful Save, **Then** the in-memory layout draft is discarded and the stored plan is unchanged. Future plantings created in Transplant View remain (they are not layout-draft objects).
13. **Given** a viewer, **When** they view Bed View, **Then** they see the last saved plantings in the bed and MAY see the tray as read-only; they cannot drag, place, direct-seed, remove from bed, or change the tray.
14. **Given** the user is using a touch screen, **When** they drag a planting in Bed View, **Then** the same place / move / transplant-return draft outcomes apply (pointer and touch are both supported).

### User Story 5 - Plan by Looking at the Garden, Not Filling Forms (Priority: P2)

Garden Overview feels like a spatial map of the real garden: beds look like beds (clear outlines, names, and relative size), non-planting areas look different from beds, and occupied beds show planting name (and count) labels. Bed View feels like looking into one bed: the rectangle matches the bed’s width and height, plantings look like plants occupying space (name visible, footprint related to spacing when known), and a side tray lists future (indoor) plantings ready to drag. Transplant View is a practical list of those indoor starts and their water and fertilizer tasks — not a cartoon greenhouse. The look is calm and practical — like a planning tool, not a cartoon illustration of vegetables.

**Why this priority**: Separate views are not enough if Overview is still a form next to anonymous boxes, or Bed View is unlabeled dots. Each view has to be readable and direct.

**Independent Test**: Open Overview with several sized beds, mixed placed plantings, at least one future planting, and a non-planting area; identify each bed and area by name from the map alone; read planting names (and counts) on occupied beds without opening Bed View; confirm Overview has no in-bed plant marks; open a bed and identify each placed planting by name from Bed View alone; confirm the tray lists only future plantings; confirm the visual language is a restrained diagram (labels and shapes, not cartoon plant art).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** sized beds and non-planting areas on Overview, **When** a member looks at the map, **Then** each bed and each non-planting area shows its name on or next to the rectangle, and each occupied bed shows planting name (and count) labels — not an unlabeled block and not position-accurate plant marks.
2. **Given** Bed View with placed plantings, **When** a member looks at the bed, **Then** each placed planting shows a readable name (not an unlabeled mark).
3. **Given** a planting with known catalog spacing, **When** it is placed in Bed View, **Then** its mark occupies a footprint that reflects that spacing so crowding is visible by eye as well as by flags.
4. **Given** future plantings, **When** an owner or collaborator views Bed View, **Then** those plantings appear in a visible tray they can drag from, each labeled with the planting’s name. Direct-seed plantings and other non-future plantings MUST NOT appear in that tray. Overview does not present that tray as a place to drop plantings onto the garden map.
5. **Given** the visual plan, **When** a participant compares it to a cartoon garden illustration, **Then** Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View are a restrained diagram or list (simple rectangles, names, and plant marks) — not illustrated cartoon vegetables or a playful illustrated scene.

### User Story 6 - The Plan Stays Responsive While Working (Priority: P2)

A gardener pans, zooms, and arranges a household-sized garden without waiting for the plan to catch up. On Overview, dragging empty map space pans the view; dragging a bed or non-planting area moves that object. Zoom controls (including pinch) change scale. In Bed View, dragging a planting moves that planting. Opening Overview shows the beds, areas, and planting name (and count) labels they already have without a long blank wait. This is the everyday garden (about 10 beds and 50 plantings), not a survey of a farm.

**Why this priority**: A visual planner that stutters while dragging is not usable. Performance is part of friendliness.

**Independent Test**: On a garden fixture with at least 10 sized beds and 50 placed plantings, open Overview, pan, zoom, and drag one bed; open a Bed View and drag one planting; gestures stay with the pointer; opening Overview does not leave the member staring at an empty map after the garden has loaded.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a garden with at least 10 sized beds and 50 placed plantings, **When** a member opens Garden Overview while online, **Then** they see those beds and planting name (and count) labels on the map without an extra wait after the garden has already loaded (the overview is ready to use in under 3 seconds from navigation for that fixture).
2. **Given** that same garden on Overview, **When** they drag on empty map space (not on a bed or non-planting area), **Then** the view pans with the gesture. **When** they zoom (controls or pinch), **Then** scale changes and rectangles stay in place relative to each other.
3. **Given** that same garden on Overview, **When** they drag a bed or non-planting area, **Then** that object moves (the view does not pan instead) and the dragged item stays under the pointer for the whole gesture.
4. **Given** Bed View, **When** they drag a planting, **Then** that planting moves with the pointer for the whole gesture.
5. **Given** a viewer, **When** they drag empty space or zoom on Overview, **Then** they can pan and zoom; they still cannot move beds, areas, or plantings.
6. **Given** a drop or resize that creates a spacing or fit problem, **When** the gesture ends, **Then** flags on the draft refresh immediately so they are visible before Save; flags MUST NOT be required to update on every pointer move during the gesture.

### Edge Cases

- Creating a bed without length or width is refused on **Create new bed**; no unsized bed is added to the garden or drawn on Overview. Creating a non-planting area without length or width is likewise refused. After a successful create, the new rectangle is in the currently visible Overview area (not off-screen, not waiting for a second click to place).
- A Save that creates new beds on the server and then fails to store geometry MUST remove those new beds **and** any direct-seed plantings created in that Save so the failed Save does not leave name-only rows.
- Name-only beds from before this rule (`geometry` null) are **deleted** by migration `0008` (direct-seed plantings on them deleted; transplants unassigned to the tray). There is no Needs size UI. After migrate, unsized beds MUST NOT exist and MUST NOT be creatable.
- A garden with future plantings but no sized beds: those plantings appear in Transplant View and in the Bed View tray once a bed exists to open; Overview shows an empty garden (or only non-planting areas) until a bed is created with a size. Direct seed requires an open Bed View (a sized bed).
- Overview MUST NOT place a planting on a bed, on a non-planting area, or in empty map space. A planting drag does not exist on Overview.
- In Bed View, dropping a planting outside the bed footprint does not place it in open space and does not delete it; a tray item remains in the tray; an already placed planting remains in its previous position unless the drop is on the tray **and** the planting is a transplant (restore to tray). The gardener is told via status / `aria-live` (e.g. **Drop missed a bed**).
- **New** future-planting records are created and fully deleted only in Transplant View. Placing a tray item onto a bed leaves the tray (it has a placement). Removing a **transplant** from a bed restores it to the tray and MUST keep all information it had (including indoor-start water and fertilizer tasks). Removing a **direct-seed** planting from a bed deletes it (after confirm). Dropping a direct-seed planting on the tray MUST NOT silently delete it.
- Non-planting areas never contain plantings. They cannot be opened as a Bed View. Selecting one on Overview selects it for geometry editing, not for planting. Confirmed delete of a non-planting area is immediate; create/move/resize of an area remain draft until Save.
- An unsaved layout draft is not what other members see and MUST NOT become the offline last successful plan. Navigating among Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View MUST NOT discard the shared layout draft. Leaving the garden planner entirely or reloading discards the in-memory layout draft. There is no separate Discard control. Future plantings created or deleted in Transplant View are planting records, not layout-draft objects.
- A planting whose catalog variety is later unavailable still shows its name (with the existing “removed from catalog” cue) and can still be moved in Bed View if it is already placed.
- Overview: dragging empty map space pans the view; it does not move beds or areas. Dragging a bed or non-planting area moves that object; it does not pan the view. Selecting a bed (click/tap without a move/resize gesture) opens Bed View; it does not open a length/width form.
- Viewers may pan and zoom on Overview, may open Bed View and Transplant View read-only; they still cannot move beds, areas, or plantings, and cannot add or delete future plantings.
- Offline: last successful saved plan remains visible on Overview and Bed View, including sized beds, non-planting areas, and placed plantings. Last successful Transplant View load remains readable. Create, drag, move, resize, rotate, delete, place, direct seed, transplant add/delete, and Save show online-required within 5 seconds and MUST NOT change the draft or the stored plan.
- Viewer: no drag handles, no tray mutations, no bed or area create/resize/delete, no planting place/direct-seed/remove-from-bed, no transplant create/full-delete.
- Unauthenticated users: no planner data; they are prompted to sign in.
- Non-members: same not-found outcome as a missing garden.
- Companion-plant suggestions, cartoon plant artwork, square-foot-only cell planting as the required model, seed-box inventory, and purchasing are out of scope.
- Direct seed in Bed View creates a planting from the plant catalog into the open bed. New transplant records are created and fully deleted only in Transplant View. Removing a transplant from a bed restores it to the tray with its information kept. Removing a direct seed from a bed deletes that planting.
- Rotate-in-90-degree-steps for beds remains an Overview geometry action (same as Garden Layout Designer). Non-planting areas in this feature are axis-aligned rectangles without a required rotate action.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Garden members MUST be able to open Garden Overview for a garden they belong to, MUST be able to open Bed View for a specific sized bed of that garden, and MUST be able to open Transplant View for that garden. Unauthenticated users MUST NOT see planner data. A non-member MUST receive the same not-found outcome as a missing garden.
- **FR-002**: Garden Overview MUST show every sized named bed that belongs to that garden on the visual map, and MUST show every non-planting area. Empty beds MUST remain visible. Beds MUST NOT be omitted merely because they have no plantings. The map MUST NOT draw unsized placeholder footprints. Occupied beds on Overview MUST show each distinct planting name, and a count when that name appears more than once in the bed. Overview MUST NOT draw plantings at in-bed positions (no plant marks on the map).
- **FR-003**: Creating a bed MUST use a **Create new bed** action on Garden Overview and MUST require a name, length, and width on that action. The new bed MUST then appear in the currently visible Overview area (for example the center of the current view) so the gardener can see it without a second click-to-place step. A create without length or width MUST be refused and MUST NOT add a bed. Unsized (`geometry` null) beds MUST NOT exist after migration `0008`: that migration deletes them (direct-seed plantings on them deleted; transplants unassigned to the tray). The API MUST refuse any later name-only bed create. A Save MUST NOT leave new name-only beds on the server: if new beds (or new direct-seed plantings) are created and the layout Save then fails, those new beds **and** those new direct-seed plantings MUST be removed.
- **FR-004**: Owners and collaborators MUST be able to add plantings to the open bed **only in Bed View**, in exactly two ways: (1) **direct seed** — choose any plant from the catalog and place it in this bed; (2) **transplant** — drag a future planting from the planting tray onto the bed. They MUST be able to reposition a placed planting within that bed. They MUST be able to **remove from bed**: a direct-seed planting MUST be deleted (after confirm); a transplant MUST return to the tray and MUST keep all information it had previously. The planting MUST follow the pointer during the drag. Pointer and touch MUST both work. Garden Overview MUST NOT add, move, or reassign plantings. Viewers MUST NOT drag, place, direct-seed, or remove from bed. Bed View MUST NOT create **new** future-planting records or fully delete transplant records (Transplant View only).
- **FR-005**: Releasing a planting drag outside the bed footprint in Bed View MUST NOT create a placement in open space and MUST NOT delete the planting. A tray item MUST remain in the tray; an already placed planting MUST remain in its previous position unless the drop is a transplant onto the planting tray (restore). The user MUST be able to tell a drop that did not land in the bed via a visible status / `aria-live` message (e.g. **Drop missed a bed**). Releasing a **transplant** on the planting tray MUST restore it to the tray with prior information kept. Releasing a **direct-seed** planting on the tray MUST NOT silently delete it. There is no Unplace control.
- **FR-006**: Placing a planting in Bed View MUST set that planting’s bed assignment on the editor’s working draft (same single set of beds and plantings as Garden Layout Designer). Spacing and fit rules, Save refusal, and attach-on-move/resize behavior MUST remain those of Garden Layout Designer: center-to-center spacing using the larger known catalog value; fit using `ceil(s / 2)` inches from each bed edge for known spacing `s`; unknown spacing marked unavailable and not blocking Save; placements stay attached when a bed moves or rotates 90 degrees on Overview; a resize on Overview that leaves a planting outside the footprint keeps it placed and flags fit; Save MUST be refused while any spacing or fit flag is present. Spacing and fit flags MUST refresh when a place, move, remove-from-bed, or resize **gesture ends**, not continuously during the pointer move. A drop that would flag MUST still land on the draft (MUST NOT snap back). A successful Save MUST then persist the draft so the planting list and other members see the same bed assignment. A refused Save MUST NOT overwrite the stored plan. Overview MUST NOT reassign plantings by dragging them between beds. Moving a transplant to another bed MUST be remove-from-bed (back to tray, information kept) then place from the tray in the destination Bed View.
- **FR-007**: Each sized bed and each non-planting area on Overview MUST show its **name** on or next to the rectangle (planting name/count labels and “no in-bed marks” are FR-002). In Bed View, each placed planting MUST show a readable name on or next to its mark, including the existing “removed from catalog” cue when the variety is later unavailable. A planting with known spacing `s` MUST occupy a footprint of radius `ceil(s / 2)` inches **in Bed View**. A planting with unknown spacing MUST occupy a visual mark of **radius 6 inches** in Bed View that MUST NOT be used as catalog spacing for flags or Save. The Bed View planting tray MUST list only future (indoor transplant) plantings that have no bed placement, each labeled with the planting’s name. Place and restore-to-tray gestures are FR-004. Viewers MUST see placed names in Bed View and MAY see the tray as read-only. Overview MUST NOT present that tray as a drop target onto the garden map.
- **FR-008**: Owners and collaborators MUST be able to move a sized bed by dragging it on Overview, resize it on Overview, and rotate it in 90-degree steps on Overview. Non-planting-area geometry is FR-014. A drag that starts on a bed or non-planting area MUST move that object, not pan. A drag that starts on empty Overview space MUST pan the view. **Zoom in** / **Zoom out** and pinch MUST change scale. **Selecting a bed MUST open Bed View** (to add or manage plantings) and MUST NOT be the path that collects length and width. Length and width MUST be collected on **Create new bed**; after create, resize MAY still change length and width. Numeric length, width, and origin MAY remain as an accessible precision alternative for **already sized** beds that MUST NOT use the same select-to-open gesture. Confirmed bed delete remains an Overview action as in Garden Layout Designer. Bed View MUST NOT create, resize, move, rotate, or delete the bed, and MUST NOT add, move, resize, or delete non-planting areas. Viewers MUST be able to pan and zoom on Overview and MUST NOT move, resize, or rotate beds or areas; they MAY select a bed to open read-only Bed View.
- **FR-009**: The visual language MUST be a restrained garden diagram on Overview and Bed View: simple rectangles, names, and plant marks, with a light measurement grid inside the bed in Bed View to help placement. Non-planting areas MUST be visually distinct from beds. Transplant View MUST be a practical list of future plantings and their indoor-start tasks, not a cartoon greenhouse. It MUST NOT use cartoon vegetable illustrations, playful illustrated scenery, or decorative characters. Color MAY distinguish plantings and area types as long as names remain the primary identifier. Overview MUST reflect real-world spatial relationships (relative position and size of beds and areas).
- **FR-010**: On a household-sized garden of at least 10 sized beds and 50 placed plantings, opening Garden Overview MUST show those beds and planting name (and count) labels within 3 seconds of navigating to it (online, after the member is already signed in). Pan (empty-space drag on Overview), zoom, bed/area drag on Overview, and planting drag in Bed View MUST keep the view or dragged item aligned with the gesture for the duration of the gesture (no delayed jump after release as the only feedback).
- **FR-011**: The last successful saved plan MUST remain readable offline on Overview and Bed View (sized beds, non-planting areas, and placements). The last successful Transplant View load MUST remain readable offline. Planner mutations (create bed, create/move/resize/delete non-planting area, drag, move, resize, rotate, confirmed delete, place, direct seed, transplant add/delete, Save) MUST require connectivity, MUST show an online-required state, and MUST NOT change the draft or the stored plan while offline. Stale cache after membership loss MUST NOT authorize new edits once the device reconnects: on reconnect, GET MUST return 404/403 and the UI MUST NOT Save or mutate (same pattern as existing garden/layout cache).
- **FR-012**: Direct seed in Bed View MUST create a planting from the plant catalog into the open bed only. Transplant (future) plantings MUST be created and deleted only in Transplant View. The feature MUST NOT create calendar plans, MUST NOT alter personal favorites, and MUST NOT add companion-planting rules, purchasing, GPS/survey mapping, non-rectangular beds or areas, or free rotation at arbitrary angles.
- **FR-013**: Planner layout edits other than confirmed bed delete and confirmed non-planting-area delete (create bed, move, resize, rotate, create/move/resize non-planting area, place, direct seed into a bed, reposition, remove-from-bed) MUST update a working draft shared by Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View navigation and visible to that editor. Confirmed bed delete MUST remain an immediate household delete (FR-008), not a draft-until-Save action. Confirmed non-planting-area delete MUST also be immediate (FR-014). Create and full-delete of future plantings in Transplant View MUST persist as planting records (not as the layout draft). An explicit Save from Overview or Bed View MUST commit the layout draft. Other members MUST see the last successful layout save until then. Navigating among Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View MUST NOT discard the layout draft. Leaving the garden planner entirely or reloading without a successful Save MUST discard the in-memory layout draft and MUST leave the stored plan unchanged (no separate Discard control). The editor MUST be able to tell that unsaved layout draft changes are not yet committed (`Unsaved changes`). Viewers have no draft and MUST NOT Save.
- **FR-014**: Non-planting areas MUST exist only on Garden Overview. Each MUST be a rectangle defined by width, height, and x/y position, visually distinct from beds. They MUST never contain plantings and MUST NOT open a Bed View. Creating one MUST require a name, length, and width, and the new area MUST appear in the currently visible Overview area (same placement as FR-003; no second click-to-place). Area names MUST be unique among areas in that garden (empty name → 400; duplicate → 409); a bed MAY share an area’s name. Owners and collaborators MUST be able to add, move, and resize them on Overview (draft until Save). Removing a non-planting area MUST require in-page confirm; after confirmation the area is gone immediately (not draft-until-Save) and MUST NOT delete beds or plantings. Canceling confirm MUST leave the area unchanged. Viewers MUST NOT mutate them.
- **FR-015**: Transplant View MUST be the only place that **creates new** future-planting records and **fully deletes** them (Bed View restore-to-tray is FR-004, not a full delete). Future plantings MUST have no bed placement while they are in the tray. Indoor water and fertilizer items MUST be **derived** from catalog intervals for unplaced transplants (`deriveIndoorReminders`); omit a kind when that interval is null. Owners and collaborators complete/dismiss those items like Care Reminders (006). That information MUST be kept when a transplant is removed from a bed back to the tray. Direct-seed plantings MUST NOT receive those indoor-start items. Viewers MUST NOT add, delete, complete, or dismiss them.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Garden Overview**: The visual, to-scale map of one garden — placed beds, non-planting areas, and planting name (and count) labels on occupied beds. This view owns bed identity and geometry.
- **Bed View**: The visual, to-scale interior of one garden bed — the bed’s width and height (not editable here) and the plantings inside it. Direct seed and placing transplants happen here. This view owns that bed’s in-ground plantings.
- **Transplant View**: The view for indoor-started future plantings: create new records, fully delete them, and complete/dismiss **derived** indoor water/fertilizer items. New tray records start here. A transplant restored from a bed also appears here and in the tray until placed again.
- **Named bed**: The same household bed as on the planting list. A bed is created only via **Create new bed** with length and width, then appears in the currently visible Overview area and is drawn to scale. After create, geometry (move, resize, rotate) is edited only on Overview; selecting the bed opens Bed View, where plantings are edited.
- **Non-planting area**: A named rectangle on Overview (walkway, path, structure, or similar) that never contains plantings and is not a bed.
- **Future planting**: An indoor-started transplant with no bed placement yet. Tracked in Transplant View; listed in the Bed View planting tray until placed.
- **Direct-seed planting**: A planting created in Bed View from the plant catalog and placed in the open bed. Never appears in the indoor tray. Does not receive indoor-start water and fertilizer tasks.
- **Planting mark**: The visible position of one seasonal planting in a bed, labeled by name, shown **only in Bed View**. Known spacing `s` uses radius `ceil(s / 2)` inches. Unknown spacing uses radius 6 inches and is not catalog spacing. Overview uses name (and count) labels instead of marks.
- **Planting tray**: The Bed View list of future plantings (no bed placement). Editors drag from it to place in the open bed. **New** records are created and fully deleted only in Transplant View. A placed transplant removed from a bed returns here and keeps its prior information. A direct-seed planting removed from a bed is deleted and never appears here.
- **Spacing / fit flag**: Unchanged meaning from Garden Layout Designer (too close, does not fit, spacing unavailable). Shown on the draft after a place/move/resize gesture ends; not required to update on every pointer move.

### Access Control *(mandatory when data is user-owned or shared)*

- **Roles / permissions**: **Owner** and **collaborator** edit Overview (beds and non-planting areas), Bed View (direct seed and placing/moving plantings), and Transplant View (future plantings and indoor complete/dismiss). **Viewer** reads Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View and MUST NOT mutate. Non-members: no access (same not-found as a missing garden). Unauthenticated users are prompted to sign in (existing garden route guards).
- **Sharing rules**: The planner is garden-shared among members (same membership as Household Gardens).
- **Isolation**: Planner of Garden A MUST NOT be visible to non-members or mixed into Garden B. Personal favorites remain session-private and are not planner objects.

### Offline / PWA Considerations *(include if feature has client behavior)*

- The last successful saved plan is readable offline for members, including viewers, on Overview and Bed View (sized beds, non-planting areas, and placements). The last successful Transplant View load is readable offline.
- Create, drag, move, resize, rotate, confirmed delete, non-planting-area edits, place, direct seed, transplant add/delete, and Save require connectivity, show an online-required state, and MUST NOT change the draft or stored plan while offline (no silent failure, no planner mutation queue).
- Offline planner read MUST NOT block other cached garden, catalog, or planting-list views.
- Stale planner after membership loss MUST NOT authorize edits once the device reconnects.

- **SC-002**: At least 90% of participants can open a bed from Overview and place a future planting from the tray into that bed by dragging in Bed View, without typing coordinates, in under 45 seconds on first try. At least 90% can direct-seed a catalog plant into the same bed on first try in under 45 seconds.

- **SC-005**: On a fixture with 10 sized beds and 50 placed plantings, a signed-in member sees those beds and planting name (and count) labels on Overview within 3 seconds of opening it online; a drag of one bed on Overview and one planting in Bed View keeps the item under the pointer until release; a drag on empty Overview space pans instead of moving a bed (100% on that fixture).

- **SC-007**: After a successful planner save online, that plan remains readable offline on Overview and Bed View; an offline attempt to drag, create, confirmed-delete, direct-seed, edit a non-planting area, or add/delete a future planting shows online-required within 5 seconds and does not change the draft or placements.

## Assumptions

- This feature improves the existing garden layout experience; it does not replace Household Gardens, Seasonal Plantings, or Garden Layout Designer’s domain rules (one set of beds and plantings, spacing/fit, roles, offline read).
- “Show the beds currently in the garden” means every sized named bed for that garden appears on Garden Overview, including empty beds. Creating a bed always uses **Create new bed** and requires length and width, so the map does not include unsized placeholder footprints. Leftover name-only beds from before this rule are **deleted** by migration `0008` (not sized on Overview).
- Selecting a bed on Overview always means open Bed View (to add plantings). Length and width are not collected by selecting the bed; they are collected on Create new bed and may later change via resize (or an accessible numeric path that does not use select-to-open). After Create new bed or creating a non-planting area, the new rectangle appears in the currently visible Overview area (for example the center of the current view); the gardener then moves it. No second click-to-place step.
- Direct seed in Bed View creates a planting from the full plant catalog into the open bed. New transplant / future plantings are created and fully deleted only in Transplant View. The Bed View tray lists only those future plantings (plus transplants restored from a bed). Placing from the tray onto a bed is a layout draft until Save; add/full-delete in Transplant View is a planting-record change, not the layout draft. Remove-from-bed (direct-seed delete or transplant restore) is a layout-draft action until Save.
- Indoor-start water and fertilizer items are **derived** from catalog intervals for unplaced transplants in Transplant View (complete/dismiss like 006). Direct-seed plantings do not receive those indoor-start items. In-ground care for placed plantings remains the existing Care Reminders feature once a planting is in a bed; this feature does not replace that list.
- Overview planting labels use the planting’s name; when more than one planting in the same bed shares that name, Overview shows a count with the name. In-bed positions exist only in Bed View.
- Bed internal layout continues the existing free-position model (inches, household tape-measure scale) with a light grid as a visual aid in Bed View. Snap-to-grid and a required square-foot or row-only planting model are not required in this feature.
- Visual inspiration from [Planter](https://planter.garden/app/gardens) is the spatial planner pattern: visible beds, a plant tray, drag onto a bed, a light grid, labeled occupants — split across Overview (map) and Bed View (in-bed placement). It is not a mandate to copy square-foot-only cells, companion-planting overlays, cartoon artwork, seed-box inventory, or Planter’s product scope.
- Primary arrangement after create is direct manipulation (drag beds and areas on Overview; rotate beds on Overview; drag plantings in Bed View). First size is the Create new bed action. Number fields stay as an accessible precision path for later geometry that MUST NOT replace select-to-open.
- “Not cartoony” means restrained diagram styling: simple shapes, readable type, modest color. Decorative illustrated plants and playful scenery are out of scope.
- Performance targets describe a typical household garden (on the order of 10 beds and 50 plantings), not commercial farm scale.
- Last-write-wins for concurrent **successful** online Saves matches Household Gardens, Seasonal Plantings, and Garden Layout Designer. Unsaved drafts are not a merge editor and MUST NOT overwrite another member’s successful save until this editor Saves.
- Drags and other layout edits (except confirmed bed delete and confirmed non-planting-area delete) are a working draft shared while navigating Overview, Bed View, and Transplant View until explicit Save. Confirmed bed delete and confirmed non-planting-area delete are immediate. Leaving the garden planner entirely or reloading discards the in-memory layout draft; switching among those three views does not. The offline last successful GET is never an unsaved draft.
- Spacing and fit flags refresh when a drag or resize gesture ends, not on every pointer move, so the item can stay under the pointer during the gesture.
- Unknown-spacing planting marks use radius 6 inches for visibility only and MUST NOT feed spacing or fit math.
- Units stay household-familiar and consistent with catalog spacing (inches) unless a later display-unit toggle is added.
- A 10-bed / 50-planting garden for SC-005 is a required **manual** fixture (quickstart steps), not an optional extra and not a Playwright timing assertion.
- Non-planting areas may sit next to or visually overlap beds; the product does not require overlap prevention in this feature. They never receive plantings regardless of overlap.
- Non-planting-area create/move/resize follow the same draft-until-Save rule as bed create/move. Confirmed delete of a non-planting area is immediate (like confirmed bed delete). It does not delete beds or plantings.
